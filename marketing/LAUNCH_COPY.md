## Founder launch announcement

Custody details often end up scattered across calendars, notes, receipts, screenshots, and memory. I built Custody Folio to give parents one calm, private place to organize the facts.

Custody Folio helps an adult record dated events, parenting time, expenses, notes, and supporting files. It brings those records into a timeline and creates clearer information for personal review or an attorney conversation.

The product works independently. The other parent does not need an account. Customers control when information is shared and can revoke attorney access.

Custody Folio does not provide legal advice or promise a court result. Its purpose is simpler and more useful: help people keep important details organized when those details are hard to manage.

Every eligible account includes 30 days of full access without a card.

## Language rules

1. Say organize, prepare, review, factual, private, and customer controlled.

2. Do not say win custody, beat your ex, court approved, admissible, guaranteed, completely secure, or tamper proof.

3. Do not make assumptions about abuse, conflict, diagnosis, motive, or legal outcome.

4. Do not ask customers to publish case details as engagement content.

5. Use customer stories only with explicit written permission and minimal identifying detail.
