# Custody Folio First Seven Days of Content

## Day 1

### Topic

Why custody details become difficult to review

### Post

Important custody details often end up spread across calendars, notes, receipts, screenshots, and memory. The first step is not writing more. It is giving every dated event and original source one consistent place to live.

Custody Folio is built for that organization work. It does not provide legal advice or promise a court result.

### Action

Download the Factual Custody Record Checklist.

## Day 2

### Topic

Facts and conclusions are different

### Post

A useful record begins with what you directly observed. Start with the date, time, action, location, or amount. Keep guesses about another person's intent separate from the event itself.

Clear facts are easier to review later.

## Day 3

### Topic

The other parent does not need an account

### Post

Not every custody organization system should depend on both parents joining. Custody Folio works as an independent private workspace. You organize your records, and you decide whether to create a report or authorize attorney access.

### Action

Join the Custody Folio launch list.

## Day 4

### Topic

A five minute weekly record routine

### Post

Once each week, check five things: missing dates, supporting files, parenting time, expenses, and unclear labels. A short regular review is usually easier than rebuilding months of details later.

### Action

Save the weekly review section from the checklist.

## Day 5

### Topic

Prepare for an attorney conversation

### Post

Organized information can make an attorney conversation easier to use. Keep dated events, original sources, expenses, and questions easy to find. Let the attorney decide what is legally important.

Custody Folio helps with organization. It does not provide legal advice.

Show a synthetic timeline becoming a report and then show the attorney access control.

## Day 6

### Topic

Privacy means control

### Post

Privacy is not a dramatic claim. It is a set of clear controls. Customers should know what is stored, who can access it, how sharing works, how access is revoked, and how information is exported or deleted.

That is the standard Custody Folio is built around.

### Action

Read the Custody Folio privacy and security information.

## Day 7

### Topic

What we are learning from customers

### Post

The best product decisions begin with listening. This week we are asking adults and family law professionals what makes custody records hard to organize, what creates trust, and what a useful first ten minutes should accomplish.

We do not want private case facts. We want to understand the process and improve it.
